A little about GRECK.

Meet MacKenzie Greckel, the artist behind GRECK. With a love for bold color, curious characters, and the art of repetition, MacKenzie creates work that finds beauty somewhere between structure and spontaneity.

Her practice

Her practice is rooted in instinct. Some ideas begin with a single mark, others with a memory, a color, or a character waiting to come to life. What follows is an exploration of the familiar made unfamiliar, where imperfections are welcomed and no two pieces tell quite the same story.

A little playful. A little peculiar. Entirely GRECK.

The artist behind GRECK.

Originally from Nebraska, MacKenzie has always been drawn to the unexpected. Her appreciation for bold color, distinctive shapes, and the often-overlooked details of everyday life has shaped an artistic practice rooted in curiosity and experimentation.

Balancing intention with instinct, her work embraces repetition, individuality, and the beauty of imperfection. From the elongated, whimsical figures of BUGS to the rhythmic, hand-rendered marks of GRECK DOTS, each collection offers a different perspective on the world around her.

While her pieces are often inspired by personal memories and observations, MacKenzie leaves room for interpretation, inviting viewers to discover their own meaning within the work.

The most interesting part of art is what you make of it.

The work · Three distinct worlds

Bugs.

A study in character.

Inspired by a childhood nickname and a fascination with the praying mantis, BUGS is a collection of elongated figures with personalities of their own. Their exaggerated proportions and playful compositions blur the line between human and insect, bringing a sense of humor and individuality to each piece.

Whether horseback riding, playing tennis, or simply existing in their own little world, no two BUGS are quite alike.

Shop Bugs

Dots.

The art of repetition.

Built one mark at a time, GRECK DOTS explores the relationship between repetition, movement, and form. Each hand-rendered dot becomes part of a larger composition, creating a rhythm that feels both intentional and organic.

Inspired by the patterns of the natural world, the collection embraces subtle variations and the beauty found in repetition. What appears simple at first reveals a much more intricate story upon closer inspection.

Color.

An instinct for the unexpected.

Color has always been a defining part of MacKenzie's creative world. Influenced by childhood memories, the changing seasons, and the natural landscapes of Nebraska, her palettes bring together bold contrasts and unexpected combinations.

From vivid, saturated hues to more understated moments, color becomes more than a visual element. It brings energy, emotion, and a sense of familiarity to her work.
