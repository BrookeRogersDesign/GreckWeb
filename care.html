Care

Made to be lived in.

Your bag is made to be lived in, loved, and carried everywhere. The acrylic paint and marker are permanent and water-resistant. Long stretches of direct sun or heat can soften or stretch the acrylic and may cause ink transfer.

To keep it looking its best:

  • Avoid long stretches of direct sunlight
  • Keep it away from excess heat (hot cars count)
  • Don't machine wash
  • If needed, gently spot clean with a damp cloth

A little greck love goes a long way.
